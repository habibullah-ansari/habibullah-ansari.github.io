Build

I code the site to be fast, clean and responsive, and send you progress updates.

Review and deliver

You review it, I make the changes you ask for, and then I hand over the final files.

Questions clients ask

Quick answers before we talk.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on how many pages and features you need. Send me the details through the contact form and I will share a clear quote before we start.

How long does a website take to build?

A simple one-page website usually takes a few days, and a multi-page website about one to two weeks. I confirm the timeline with you before starting.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. I build every page mobile-first with Flexbox and Grid, then check it on phone, tablet and desktop sizes.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Share your current website link and tell me what you want to improve. I can make it cleaner, faster and responsive on every screen.

Can I request changes after delivery?

Yes. Each project includes a review round, and you can contact me later for further changes.

What do you need from me to start?

Your business name, logo, text and photos if you have them, and a few example sites you like. If you don’t have text yet, tell me about your business and we can plan it together.

How can I contact you to start a project?

Use the contact form below, or message me on WhatsApp or email. Tell me about your business and what you need, and I will reply with the next steps.
